## Conference findings
- ICLR 2025 was the main venue: SORRY-Bench, AIR-Bench 2024, AgentHarm, and ASB all appeared there. No awards for these were found.
- NeurIPS 2025 D&B: only JBDistill was surfaced; my search for the full track was weak, so coverage is incomplete.
- USENIX Security / S&P / CCS / NDSS / ACL / EMNLP 2025: searches found no award-winning safety benchmark. SoK: Evaluating Jailbreak Guardrails (arXiv 2506.10597) and GuidedBench (2502.16903) appeared but venues were not verified. Gap in my coverage; treat as unexplored.

## Blog/researcher signals
- Anthropic, Agentic Misalignment (Jun 2025): popularized agentic stress-testing for insider-threat behavior.
- Anthropic/OpenAI cross-lab alignment evaluation (27 Aug 2025): each ran its own misalignment evals on the other's models (sycophancy, whistleblowing, self-preservation, misuse, oversight undermining); SHADE-Arena sabotage was reported in coverage.
- UK AISI: Inspect Evals repository (community benchmarks including safeguards and cybersecurity), a Feb 2025 paper on principles for evaluating misuse safeguards, and agent-evaluation posts (Jul/Aug 2025). AISI renamed to AI Security Institute on 14 Feb 2025.
- CAIS newsletter (AISN #52) covered VCT. International AI Safety Report (Jan 2025): not verified in this research.

## News/product adoption
- Claude Opus 4 / Sonnet 4 system card (May 2025): ASL-3 for Opus 4; covers usage-policy tests, prompt injection, reward hacking, Apollo findings of blackmail/self-exfiltration. A third-party summary cites StrongREJECT figures (unverified against the card).
- OpenAI o3/o4-mini system card (Apr 2025, first under Preparedness Framework v2) and GPT-5 system card: SecureBio-run VCT reported for GPT-5 (41.4 +/- 1.7%).
- MLCommons AILuminate: launched publicly Dec 2024; 2025 paper. I found no verified 2025 adoption data.
- Future of Life Institute's AI Safety Index (Nov 2025) includes a dangerous-capability evaluations indicator (found, not read in depth).

## State of development
2025 shifted safety evaluation from static single-turn prompt sets toward three fronts: (1) taxonomy- and regulation-grounded harm coverage (AIR-Bench, SORRY-Bench, AILuminate), reflecting demand from policy and industry consortia; (2) agentic safety (AgentHarm, ASB, Agentic Misalignment, SHADE-Arena) where harm arises from multi-step tool use and misalignment, not single responses; and (3) frontier-risk capability and honesty evaluations (VCT, MASK) that feed lab system cards and responsible-scaling decisions.

Influence in 2025 came less from new academic datasets than from system cards and cross-lab evaluations that embedded existing ones. Many headline ICLR benchmarks were 2024 preprints. Evaluation methodology (LLM judges, renewable benchmarks) became a topic in itself.

## Gaps / areas for improvement
- Mostly English, single-turn (AILuminate itself notes single-turn and judge-uncertainty limits).
- Saturation and contamination of static benchmarks (motivation for JBDistill).
- Judge reliability: AILuminate's later jailbreak work reported judge false-negative and false-positive rates of 16.4% and 37.9% (secondary French coverage, unverified).
- Construct validity: a 2026 audit argues agent-safety benchmarks can conflate safety with capability (arXiv 2607.28685, not read).
- Simulation-only misalignment evidence; unclear real-world transfer.
- My search coverage of 2025 security/NLP venues is thin.

## Future directions
Renewable/adaptive benchmarks, multilingual and multi-turn extensions (AILuminate planned French, Chinese, Hindi), jailbreak-augmented standards, agent and sabotage evaluations in tool environments, honesty/deception and scheming evals, and regulation-linked evaluation (AIR-Bench style), plus validity audits of safety benchmarks.
