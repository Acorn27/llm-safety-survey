## State of development
2022 is when LLM safety evaluation shifted from static toxicity prompts (RealToxicityPrompts, 2020) to adversarial, human- and model-generated red teaming, and to measuring truthfulness and emergent behaviors. Datasets were largely small-scale, English-only, and tied to dialogue or RLHF training pipelines; TruthfulQA and ToxiGen were the main reusable static benchmarks, and HELM supplied the first multi-metric harness including toxicity.

## Gaps / areas for improvement
- No standardized jailbreak or misuse/dangerous-capability benchmark; red-team data not a fixed benchmark and attack coverage unmeasured.
- English-centric; judges were weak classifiers (e.g., offensive-content classifiers); truthfulness via multiple choice/GPT-judge.
- Prompt injection tested only on GPT-3 with handcrafted attacks; no agent safety.

## Future directions
Automated red teaming (Perez) led to later attack/benchmark suites; Anthropic red-team and model-written evals fed dangerous-capability and sycophancy evals; HELM-style harnesses and TruthfulQA/ToxiGen became staples; PromptInject foreshadowed indirect prompt-injection benchmarks.
