# 2020

Honest note: 2020 predates "LLM safety benchmarking" as a field. Candidates are GPT-2/BERT-era toxicity, bias, ethics and privacy-leakage resources. Venue/author facts below were verified by search; influence claims marked "unverified" were not checked this session.

## Conference findings
- NLP: RTP (Findings of EMNLP 2020), CrowS-Pairs (EMNLP 2020) and Social Bias Frames (ACL 2020) are the relevant items. I found no award for any of them.
- ML: ETHICS went to ICLR 2021 (submitted 2020). NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks did not exist yet (it started in 2021).
- Security: Carlini et al. was posted in Dec 2020 and appeared at USENIX Security 2021. I did not exhaustively search 2020 S&P, CCS or NDSS for LLM-specific work and found nothing. Jailbreak and prompt-injection benchmarks did not exist.
- I did not search every 2020 workshop paper, so coverage of the long tail is incomplete.

## Blog/researcher signals
Not searched in depth. From background knowledge, which is not verified here: OpenAI's GPT-3 paper (May 2020) discussed bias and toxicity qualitatively. The Perspective API was the de facto toxicity scorer. I found no 2020 blog from Anthropic, CAIS or Lilian Weng that highlights these benchmarks. Weng's toxicity post is from 2021 (unverified).

## News/product adoption
- ParlAI released the BAD classifiers publicly, and Facebook's BlenderBot work used the safety recipes. The direct link to product use is not verified.
- No government or regulatory evals used these benchmarks in 2020, as far as I found.
- Adoption of RTP in model cards came in later years (unverified here; the 2021+ researchers should check).

## State of development
In 2020, LLM safety evaluation was mostly toxicity and social bias on GPT-2/BERT-class models. RealToxicityPrompts gave the first large-scale prompted toxic-degeneration test, and Meta's BAD work introduced adversarial human-in-the-loop safety data. ETHICS started value-alignment benchmarking. Evaluation relied on automatic classifiers such as Perspective, which have known biases.

Security-style concerns such as privacy and memorization appeared as attacks (Carlini) rather than benchmarks. Jailbreaks, misuse and dangerous-capability evals, truthfulness and agent safety were absent.

## Gaps / areas for improvement
- Toxicity scoring depends on a single classifier.
- Bias benchmarks have validity problems (CrowS-Pairs noise).
- English only, with no instruction-following or chat models, no adversarial robustness, and no dangerous-capability or truthfulness evals.
- No standard harness.

## Future directions
- RTP led to toxicity evaluation in model reports (HELM and similar, unverified).
- BAD led to red-teaming datasets.
- ETHICS led to MACHIAVELLI/MMLU-style value benchmarks from the same group (unverified).
- Carlini led to training-data extraction research.
- TruthfulQA (2021) is the next obvious step.
